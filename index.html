performance-ai

A private tool that helps its owner, a performance-marketing specialist, report on and manage the advertising accounts they are authorized to access.

What it does

  • Reads advertising results (spend, clicks, conversions) from Google Ads and other ad platforms, and website statistics from Google Analytics.
  • Builds reports and highlights problems when the owner asks for them through the owner's AI assistant.
  • Prepares changes, such as pausing an ad or adjusting a budget, and applies them only as authorized by the owner.

Who uses it

performance-ai is not a public service and has no sign-up. It runs on its owner's computer and is used only by its owner.

Data

How performance-ai accesses, uses, stores and shares data from Google APIs is described in the Privacy Policy.
