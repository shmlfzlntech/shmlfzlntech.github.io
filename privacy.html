Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how performance-ai (“the app”) accesses, uses, stores and shares data, including data received from Google APIs. The app is a private tool operated and used by one person, its owner, to work with advertising accounts the owner is authorized to access. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

The app asks for two Google permissions:

  • Google Ads (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords): the Google Ads accounts available to the owner, their campaign structure and settings, performance statistics (impressions, clicks, cost, conversions), search terms and change history. This permission also allows changes to campaigns, for example pausing an ad or adjusting a budget. The app makes such changes only as authorized by the owner: either the owner approves the change, or it falls within limits the owner has set in advance.
  • Google Analytics, read-only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly): aggregated website reports, such as sessions, users and conversions, for properties available to the owner.

The app does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the app’s features to the owner: reports, analysis, finding problems, preparing changes and applying the changes the owner has authorized. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to build profiles of people, and not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner’s computer.
  • Report results and a log of changes may be kept in a local database on the same computer.
  • The app has no servers of its own. Google user data is not uploaded to any server operated by the app’s developer.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with anyone, with one exception: when the owner asks a question in their AI assistant (Anthropic’s Claude), the app passes the requested results to that assistant so it can answer. This happens only at the owner’s request. Data may also be disclosed if required by law.

Limited Use

performance-ai’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it and can be deleted from the owner’s computer at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the app can no longer access Google data.

Changes to this policy

Updates are published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted as an issue at github.com/shmlfzlntech/shmlfzlntech.github.io/issues.
